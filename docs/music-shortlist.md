# Classical music shortlist

Calm, steady classical pieces that could be included as music, found on Wikimedia Commons on 5 November 2026.

## How they were chosen

- **Public domain:** every recording was made for Musopen, a US non-profit, by real performers and released into the public domain. Musopen asks for a credit as a courtesy. Each file page on Commons states the licence.
- **Human-made:** all were recorded in the 2010s by named musicians or Musopen's own ensembles.
- **Suited to focus:** each is a slow, steady movement without long loud climaxes or stormy sections. Each is at least 5 minutes long, and the full list runs to about 2 hours 48 minutes.
- **Left out:**
  - dramatic slow movements, such as the Rachmaninoff Piano Concerto No. 2 Adagio, the "New World" Largo and the "Eroica" funeral march
  - movements with violent middle sections, such as Schubert's D959 Andantino
  - old commercial recordings uploaded from the European Archive, whose public domain status in the UK is unclear

Nobody has listened to these yet. Lloyd should check each one for recording quality, sudden loud passages and how it sits under the breath sounds.

## Before using them

- Listen to each one, and drop any with poor sound or sudden loud passages.
- Confirm the licence on each file page again at the time of download.
- Serve them from the app's own host, so listeners' devices don't contact Wikimedia, and so they can work offline. Whether they can be committed to this public repo is Lloyd's decision under the third-party audio rule in `CLAUDE.md`.
- Credit Musopen and the performers on the About page.
